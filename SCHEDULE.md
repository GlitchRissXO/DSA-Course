## On slipping

You will miss sessions. Everyone does, and the schedule is built expecting it.

**Rules for falling behind:**

1. **Never skip a session to catch up.** Shift the whole calendar right instead. Skipped foundations compound — Module 07 is unteachable without Module 04.
2. **A missed day costs you one day, not the plan.** Slide everything forward and carry on.
3. **If you slip more than a full week, tell me and we re-cut the schedule.** A schedule you're constantly failing is worse than an honest slower one.
4. **Weekends stay off.** If you're using them to catch up, the pace is wrong — say so and we'll drop to 6 or 7 sessions a week. Finishing in ten weeks beats quitting in four.

The end date is a target, not a deadline. Nobody is grading you on Oct 9.
